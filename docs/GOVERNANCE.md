# Governance

The platform is designed for investment research and decision support, not autonomous trading.

## Human-in-the-Loop Controls

- Risk profile selection
- Suitability checks
- Warning messages under abnormal market conditions
- Manual override notes
- Explicit human confirmation before accepting a research recommendation
- Downloadable audit report

## Research Boundary

The platform does not:

- place trades
- connect to brokers
- provide personalized financial advice
- guarantee performance
- replace compliance or suitability review

## Responsible Use

Any production extension must add separate controls for data licensing, access management, model validation, compliance review, and operational risk. The current application is an auditable research platform whose outputs require independent human judgment.
